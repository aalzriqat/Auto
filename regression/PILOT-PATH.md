## Gaps (pilot-path steps with no backend test asserting the outcome)

1. **The pilot cash flow is pay in full by deposit, then complete** (SCRUM-802, owner ruling Option A): quote -> `deposits.create` for the full price (the quote's vehicle price) -> `sales.create` / `completeFromQuote`, which applies the deposit to the invoice (Dr 2100 / Cr 1200) so the receivable is zero. A cash completion that would leave the invoice open is refused server-side, and only DEPOSITS count toward "paid in full" (see gap 1b), and an unlinked customer receipt or cheque is refused while the customer owes a cash sale invoice. The old complete-then-collect flow (`sales.create` unpaid -> `collections.recordPayment` on the sale's own receivable) is NOT the pilot route; it stays disabled until the receipt-application lifecycle is certified (post-pilot, SCRUM-813). Pinned by `convex/scrum802CashSaleContainment.test.ts`, `convex/ownedSaleTaxEndToEnd.test.ts` (record above) and the CASH cells of `convex/scrum486Certification.test.ts`. Still not covered: a browser run of the wizard's Record Deposit then Submit Sale path (`playwright/tests/sales.spec.ts` was re-cut here but not executed in this change) and a real-backend rehearsal of the re-cut M486C1/M486C2/SR1 cases (needs the exact-SHA cloud rehearsal).
1a. **Taxed cash sales, and cash sales with fees, a warranty or GAP, are not completable in the pilot, are not offered at launch, and the server refuses them** (SCRUM-802, owner rulings 2026-10-19: uncertified money paths are blocked on the server, not just documented). A deposit is capped at the quote's `vehiclePrice`, but such an invoice is larger than the price, so it can never be held in full as a deposit. Every CASH completion door (`sales.create`, `sales.completeDraft`, and the `completeSale` / `completeExistingSale` choke point behind `completeFromQuote`) now refuses it with the specific code `CASH_SALE_TAX_FEES_WARRANTY_REFUSED` (AR and EN text), after tenant auth and before any write and before `runWithIdempotency`, so a refused call leaves no idempotency row either (full-table snapshot unchanged). `completeFromQuote` carries no tax, fee or warranty by construction (a quote has none and the door passes none); a test pins that and the helper-level refusal. The switch is the compile-time constant `CASH_SALE_EXTRAS_PILOT_REFUSED` in `convex/utils/saleDebtContainment.ts` (active while the full-payment switch is on), pinned ON, with an end-to-end mutation attack (`convex/scrum802CashSaleExtrasSwitchAttack.test.ts`: flip it off and the refusal cells go red). Pinned by `convex/scrum802CashSaleExtras.test.ts` (failing-first per door and per kind: tax amount, tax rate, dealer fee, warranty, GAP). Launch scope is untaxed cash sales with no fees, no warranty and no GAP. GAP is refused with the others as a coordinator scope call (it enlarges the invoice the same way), not an owner ruling: the owner's answer covered tax, fees and a warranty. `convex/ownedSaleTaxEndToEnd.test.ts` was re-cut: the regression record `cash-sale-with-tax-is-refused-with-the-specific-code-before-any-write` is bound to its refusal cell, and the old record `cash-taxed-owned-sale-gl-subledger-invoice-agree` is RETIRED by ruling SCRUM-802#c23551 (the owner's three decision-card answers of 2026-10-19, recorded in Jira and snapshotted in `regression/rulings.json`). RETIRED with the reason recorded in that test file's header, not left skipped: the three cells that proved a completed taxed cash sale posts one amount to both books, because they only ran by inflating the quote's `vehiclePrice` to price plus tax, a setup no user can reach. The taxed posting arithmetic stays pinned at the unit level (`accounting/ownedSaleTaxPosting.test.ts`). This is a known pilot limit and a post-pilot activation gate: offering taxed cash sales, or cash sales with fees, a warranty or GAP, needs a deposit-cap change, a new certification round and the owner's approval.
1b. **Trade-in settlement of a cash sale is not offered in the pilot** (SCRUM-802, owner ruling 2026-10-19 "deposit only"). Only deposits count toward pay-in-full-first; a cash sale paid only by a trade-in allocation, or by a part deposit plus a trade-in, is refused server-side with `CASH_SALE_BALANCE_UNPAID_REFUSED`, with nothing written. The rule lives in one named constant, `TRADE_IN_COUNTS_TOWARD_CASH_PAY_FIRST` (`convex/utils/cashSalePilotGate.ts`), pinned `false` by its own test. It is read only by the exact post-allocation gate; the read-only pre-write check counts deposits only by construction and never reads it, so flipping the constant alone does not enable trade-in settlement. A post-pilot activation gate: counting a trade-in needs a real rule change (the pre-write check included), a new certification round and the owner's approval. The pay-first refusal (trade-in case included) is raised BEFORE the first sale write on every completion path: a read-only check (`assertCashSaleDepositsPayInvoiceBeforeWrites`) derives, without writing, what the deposit resolution would apply to the car's invoice (only the car's own held deposit; a stated REFUND_TO_CUSTOMER, FORFEITED or OTHER treatment, a forfeited or refunded deposit and a trade-in apply nothing) and refuses on the exact unpaid balance. It runs in `completeSale`, `completeExistingSale` and, for every car up front, `completeSalesForLineItems`; the post-allocation gate (`assertCashSaleInvoiceFullyPaid`) stays as the backstop on the actual invoice balance. The idempotency claim row at the three door mutations is written first and rolled back by the transaction; the extras refusal sits before `runWithIdempotency`. Pinned by `convex/scrum802CashSaleContainment.test.ts`, `convex/scrum802CashSaleExtras.test.ts` and `convex/scrum802CashSaleOrdering.test.ts` (a write-recording db and scheduler record zero writes on each refused path, including a fully deposited sale whose deposit is stated as refunded, forfeited or OTHER, a deposit already refunded or forfeited, and a part deposit; mutation-checked: disabling the pre-check turns all 17 refusal cells red, and the 3 control cells that complete a sale stay green by design).
2. **No owned cash sale pins the whole journal by account** (cash/AR, revenue, COGS, inventory) with literal numbers. Tax, COGS and the consigned entry are pinned separately.
3. **Cash delivery has no discrete mutation**; HANDOVER is a rail state tied to COMPLETED, so only reader state is tested.
4. **No single financed test chains** quote -> approval -> handover -> finalize -> forward -> `confirmDisbursement` -> reports. Pieces are spread over four files.
5. **Financed revenue/margin on the P&L report (not the commission base) for an owned car is pinned only by v1 pure tests and cockpit readers**; no v2 P&L parity test. The v1 posting-plan tests pin a superseded rule (stale-risk).
6. **Configured-company route with a customer gap through `finalizeDeal` plus the customer's real `recordPayment` on the gap invoice**: untested (gap-in-cash journal pinned on the manual route only).
7. **`confirmDisbursement` ledger outcome** on a real v2 deal is pinned via financeCompanyForward / SCRUM-241, but accountingPhase8's own test is seeded and asserts no ledger.
8. **Cash-sale permission denial is thin**: no test of `sales.create` refused for a role without the permission.
9. **Cash cancel**: covered only by the SCRUM-704 matrix (`describe.each`, so not bindable one record per case yet) and accountingPhase8; no cash-payment refund.
10. **Cash P&L parity beyond revenue 15,000 -> 0** (margin, COGS) not asserted.
11. **Weak existing tests**: accountingPhase2 (4-line count only), accountingPhase0 idempotency (row counts only), sales.test ledger-transaction test (legacy row only). Not recorded; strengthening them is its own `convex/` PR.
