# Treat the finance company's approved financing amount as an entered figure

The finance company approves an amount of money. The approval records that amount (F, integer minor units in the organisation currency) and it is the funded authority. Percentages shown to the user are derived from F and never recompute it. [SCRUM-613](https://autoflowdealer.atlassian.net/browse/SCRUM-613), reference case D-000006 in [SCRUM-993](https://autoflowdealer.atlassian.net/browse/SCRUM-993).

Funded equals F. The customer applied amount is the first payment capped at the approved purchase value A. The dealership contribution is `max(0, A - F - applied)`. The excess is `max(0, F + applied - A)`, paid above A, and the default is that it raises dealer profit (it appears as an `APPROVAL_EXCESS` management-profit line). The identity is `funded + applied + contribution - excess = A`. For D-000006 (quotation 11,000, A 10,500, F 9,450, customer 1,240) funded is 9,450, contribution 0, excess 190, and the dealer receives 10,690. Measured from the quotation the displayed contribution is 310. This is display only: the cash contribution to the finance company stays on the approved-value basis.

The writer `approveDealerPurchaseAmount` takes an optional `approvedFinancingAmountMinor`, refuses zero, negative, fractional, non-finite, or above A, and stores it with the approval. A re-approval without it, `reopenApproval`, and a superseding appraisal clear it. Rows approved before this change keep the percentage basis until re-approved. There is no backfill and no production write. Closed, handed-over and posted deals keep their stored figures because nothing recomputes them. The approval form requires the amount and shows the derived percentages; an amount above the configured percentage is accepted with a notice.

Per-deal options for the excess are not built because none are specified. The default is the only behaviour. The ledger posting plans forward the whole deposit and have no excess leg, so `finalizeDeal` refuses an approval with an excess, which fails closed instead of posting a wrong figure. Making the validator itself required is a follow-up: about 150 existing callers pass no amount, and the refusal today is the form plus the value checks.

To roll back, stop passing the amount from the form. Rows without it behave exactly as before.
