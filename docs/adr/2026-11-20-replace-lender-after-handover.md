# Keep the deal and delivery when replacing its lender

An open financed deal may use an accepted quote from another configured lender for the same customer, vehicle, price and currency. The first release covers a dealership-owned vehicle with zero first payment and no recorded payments, invoice, forward transfer, fees or active unwind. The owner approved preserving the deal number and original delivery while obtaining new approval for the affected evidence. [SCRUM-966 owner ruling](https://autoflowdealer.atlassian.net/browse/SCRUM-966?focusedCommentId=25472)

A request records the observed agreement revision and delivery event. It places closing evidence on hold while retaining the original agreement. A finance approver with finance and finance-application visibility who is neither the requester nor the salesperson may apply the correction, recording the replacement lender's confirmation. Withdrawal restores the original evidence path. The approval checks both quote identities, current authority and outstanding financial dependencies again.

The existing deal and vehicle commitment continue under the replacement quote. The original quote remains historical and cannot be used for another conversion or deposit request. The recorded delivery and original approval remain attributable, and the same application receives the replacement lender's rules. The ordinary financing steps must supply a newly recorded quotation and purchase approval before closing. A zero first payment on the new agreement still requires those records. The approval moves the current references and clears superseded authority in one transaction. [Convex transaction atomicity](https://docs.convex.dev/database/advanced/occ)

The correction itself posts no money. Existing settlement, finalization and cancellation paths remain authoritative. A financial reset refuses a surviving correction that references a deal or quote it would remove. Full organization deletion drains these records before their source rows and preserves the existing deletion cursor.
