# Keep accounting workflow authority in the existing mutations

SCRUM-963 moves accounting event adapters behind the existing `workflowHooks.ts` exports. The mutation that owns a business event remains responsible for organization membership, role and source ownership, and passes its existing `MutationCtx` directly to the adapter. The adapter preserves the event tuple, source proof, currency and period decisions, outbox and ledger ordering, and the specific reversal authority. Receipt authority stays with the original trusted occurrence and retained-credit entry points. Raw `postOrEnqueue` remains private to those entry points and the domain-event posting helper. Existing registered functions, callers, permissions, and accounting policy carry forward. The chart readers, legacy account remappers, generic provisioning, and outbox enqueue/cancel helpers are ordinary backend modules so the adapters can use them through the protected import boundary. Chart management and outbox worker registrations stay in their original files. The economic command census follows named exports to the actual adapter so moving files preserves its coverage.
